Hurdle Jump Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Hurdle Jump ("the game") is made by Philip Casper ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. We keep it short and plain, because the game collects very little.

The short version

  • You don't create an account. The game makes a random player ID on your device.
  • If you choose a player name, it is shown publicly on the leaderboard, along with your best score, profile picture choice and game stats.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your information.
  • You can delete everything at any time from Options → Data & Privacy → Delete my data.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID.

Multiplayer races

During a race, your player name (or player number, if you haven't chosen a name), your runner's outfit and your moves are passed between the players in that race through our server. Race data is not stored after the race ends.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, unlocks, outfits and settings are saved on your device so the game remembers them. They stay on your device unless they are part of the leaderboard information listed above.

Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising. We use these service providers to run the game's online features:

  • Render (render.com) hosts the game's server. Like most hosts, it may keep short-term technical logs that include IP addresses.
  • Upstash (upstash.com) stores the leaderboard information described above.
  • If you play the web version on itch.io, itch.io's own privacy policy also applies to their website.
  • If the game offers purchases, they are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

  • Leaderboard entries, names, profiles and medals: until you delete them, or until we reset the leaderboard.
  • Daily Challenge runs: a few days (the daily leaderboard resets every day).
  • The short-term record used to limit abuse: about one minute.
  • Hosting logs: as kept by our hosting provider, usually a short period.

Deleting your information

In the game, open Options → Data & Privacy → Delete my data. This deletes your name, scores, stats, profile and daily results from our server and erases everything on your device. It can't be undone. Each device you've played on has its own player ID, so delete on each device you used.

If you can't use the game, email us at gamesmadebyphil@gmail.com and we'll help.

Names and reports

Player names are checked against a filter for offensive words. You can report a name you think is offensive; reports are reviewed by us and offensive names are removed.

Children

Hurdle Jump is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to how it's used. Because we don't know who you are, the simplest way to exercise these rights is the in-game Delete my data option or by emailing us with your player name or number.

Where information is processed

Our service providers may store and process information in the United States or other countries. By playing online, you understand your information may be processed there.

Security

Information is sent to our server over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect to a minimum.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game or on its store page.
